# Lattice community standards

Lattice is a technical community for people running, studying, and building an open network. Participation does not require a legal name, biography, employer, location, or public identity.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims and implementations with evidence.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Help newcomers reproduce results rather than dismissing them.
- Respect pseudonymity and do not investigate or disclose another participant's identity.
- Keep disagreements focused on the work.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect a technical proposal.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sustained personal attacks
- Doxxing or attempts to connect pseudonymous participants to private identities
- Publishing private messages or security reports without permission
- Spam, impersonation, undisclosed promotion, or market manipulation
- Knowingly false technical or financial claims
- Encouraging unsafe use while concealing known limitations

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community and its technical record. Moderation decisions should identify the violated standard without exposing unnecessary personal information.

For ordinary moderation concerns, use the reporting or moderator tools on the platform where the conduct occurred. Do not post another participant's private information to make a report. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
