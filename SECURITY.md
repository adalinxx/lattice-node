# Security policy

Do not publish an unpatched vulnerability in an issue, discussion, chat room, or social post.

## Reporting

Use [GitHub's private vulnerability report](https://github.com/adalinxx/lattice-node/security/advisories/new) for vulnerabilities affecting the node, consensus, networking, wallet interoperability, public APIs, or hosted network infrastructure. Private vulnerability reporting is enabled on the repository; GitHub may require you to sign in before opening the report form.

Include, when possible:

- the affected repository, revision, and configuration;
- the security property that fails;
- reproduction steps or a minimal test;
- expected impact and prerequisites;
- whether the issue is already being exploited or publicly discussed; and
- a safe way to contact you, which may be pseudonymous.

Do not include private keys, node cookies, seed phrases, or credentials. Replace them with synthetic test values.

## Response

Maintainers will validate the report, coordinate a fix across affected repositories and operators, and publish details once users have a reasonable opportunity to update. A report may be closed as non-security-sensitive when it has no confidentiality, integrity, availability, consensus, or asset-safety impact.

There is currently no guaranteed response-time or bounty program. Reports are evaluated on technical merit, not the identity of the reporter.
